<table>
<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>Q.</td><td>How much is it to walk one dog?</td></tr>
<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>A.</td><td>£15 per hour for a group walk. £20 per hour for a solo walk.</td></tr>

<tr><td>Q.</td><td>What if I have more than one dog to walk? </td></tr>
<tr><td>A.</td><td>Each additional dog from the same household is £10 per hour, solo or group walk.</td></tr>

<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>Q.</td><td>What is the difference between a solo and group walk for my dog?</td></tr>
<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>A.</td><td>Solo is for dogs who need to be walked alone, or with dogs from the same household. These are suitable for dogs who are reactive or just dogs who don't wish to walk with other dogs, giving you and the dogs peace of mind.
<br/>
Group walks are suitable for dogs who love to mix with other dogs and play. They need to be friendly, walk well on a lead and listen to you. Mustn't be reactive to other dogs if they find food, sticks, or toys while on a walk
<br/>
Spaces are limited for solo and group walks. When taking on new clients (both solo and group walks), we start on a trial basis to make sure everyone is settled and happy.</td></tr>

<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>Q.</td><td>What area do you cover? </td></tr>
<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>A.</td><td>If you are in Laneast, Tresmeer, Altarnun, Lewannick or in Launceston, then we can help, providing we have space available. </td></tr>

<tr><td>Q.</td><td>Do you have insurance?</td></tr>
<tr><td>A.</td><td>Yes. We have dog walking and public liability insurance.</td></tr>

<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>Q.</td><td>Do you do overnight pet sits?</td></tr>
<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>A.</td><td>No. We have stopped doing these, but there are other dog walkers in the area who may be able to help. </td></tr>

<tr><td>Q.</td><td>Do you do home boarding?</td></tr>
<tr><td>A.</td><td>No. A dog boarding licence is necessary for this which we do not have - when looking at dog boarding, do make sure they have a home boarding licence.</td></tr>

<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>Q.</td><td>What is a reactive dog?</td></tr>
<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>A.</td><td>A reactive dog, for example, could be a dog who dislikes other dogs and can lunge nip or bite other dogs, or growl and fight another dog over a stick, toy or food, despite normally being friendly with other dogs. In this situation, it's probably best having solo walks or with other dogs from the same household.</td></tr>

<tr><td>Q.</td><td>What is the benefit of the (free) meet and greet?</td></tr>
<tr><td>A.</td><td>We do this so we can meet yourselves and of course your dog. It gives you a chance to ask any questions and for you to show us where to find your dogs lead, collar, etc. We'll ask you to complete a form with all your dogs details and ask to sign our terms and conditions. We will also show you our qualifications and insurance details.</td></tr>

<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>Q.</td><td>What happens if my dog comes into season?</td></tr>
<tr style="background:#EEEEEE"><td>A.</td><td>We won't be able to walk your dog, but we can offer pop-ins while your dog is in season.</td></tr>

</table>
