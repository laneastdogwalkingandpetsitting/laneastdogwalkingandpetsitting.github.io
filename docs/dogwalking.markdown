When we walk your dog, we will walk it for an hour, either somewhere near your home, or if you are happy for us to drive your dog, we can take it to somewhere your dog will enjoy. Our vehicles are equipped with crates of various sizes to match different sized dogs.

In Summer, on a hot day, we prefer walking your dog under tree cover so there is shade and ideally with a stream/river where your dog is not going to get too hot and can take a dip if they wish. In any case, during hot weather, we take water out with us so we can give your dog a drink.

During exceptionally hot weather, we either walk dogs early in the morning, or cancel walks entirely (no cancellation charge applies) and pop-ins can be arranged instead (normal pop-in rates apply).

Sometimes we will walk your dog with other dogs, called group walks, but first we check to ensure that all dogs in a group have the correct behaviour and temperament to do so.

# Group walks
Do you have a dog that has good recall and no behaviour problems? If so, your dog could join one of our group walks. I'll arrange a meet and greet with you where I'll assess your dog(s) and if I think it will fit in with one of my current groups, I'll take it on, on a trial basis. If all goes well, your dog becomes part of our dog walking family. I will of course keep you updated on how they are getting on.

If you have a dog(s) that you think would benefit from a group walk, please do [get in touch](/contactus/). There is limited availability.

# Solo walks
Solo walks are mainly offered by Rich, due to Hannah busy with the group walks. These are tailored for those dogs who prefer to go out on their own, or with dogs from the same household only for what ever reason.

If you have a dog that needs a solo walk, please [get in touch](/contactus/), however there is also limited availability.
