Blog post: Follow your dream (please click here)

When I was little I always used to get asked what I wanted to do when I was grown up.  My answer would be working with animals.  I think most people thought yeah, yeah.

Well let me tell you wasn't till I was around 38 that I started my own dog walking business.  We are almost 4 years in now and only a few months into the business my husband joined me as well.

As I left school I went through so many jobs, not coz I was lazy but I either hated them, wasn't enough hours or life just threw curve balls.

What I want to say is never ever give up on your dream.  When the time is right it will happen.  You will need to work for it.  Believe in yourself,  so many years I felt worthless coz I was rubbish at school, turns out I was just built a different way.  Animals are my passion and my everything and for once I can honestly say I'm happy and truly love what I do.  

So I'd like to thank everyone for trusting me with your pets because without you I'd not be fulfilling my dream.

Don't ever give up,  go chase that dream and most importantly Believe in yourself as once you do that's when it becomes reality.  

Thanks for reading and good luck in whatever life brings you. 

All the best Hannah. X
