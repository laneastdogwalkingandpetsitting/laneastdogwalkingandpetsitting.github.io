Hi everyone.

Walking our clients dogs when in season has become difficult for us now that we are much busier. We used to be able to juggle things to make it work, but we've reached the point where most times we can't do this.

In an ideal world, we'd solo walk dogs in season, somewhere that we wouldn't come across other dogs but most of the time this isn't feasible for us. Walking them with, or where we would come across male dogs is likely to cause a lot of disruption and upset to other dogs and their owners.

The best we can do if your dog comes into season, is to do pop-in visits (we will unfortunately need to charge you the normal dog walking rate) for them to do a toilet break in your garden or we skip walking them altogether for 3 weeks if you can find alternative arrangements, eg a family member to pop in. Then we resume walking once sure the season is completely finished.  

We completely understand that this may not suit everyone and we apologise for the inconvenience this may cause. We would like our clients to consider getting female dogs spayed if your not planning to breed with them to avoid these difficulties and ask your vet about the health benefits as well for not having anymore seasons. 🙂 

Many thanks for your understanding.

All the best Hannah & Rich
