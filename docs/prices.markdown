We try to keep our prices as transparent as possible. We have increased our additional dog from the same household charge starting January 2026, bringing it in line with other dog walkers in the area. Unfortunately our costs are creeping up (insurance, vehicle costs, etc) so unfortunately we needed to make a price increase to cover this.

For customers within 10 mile-ish radius of us, we charge £15 per hour and £12.50 for pop in visits (usually lasting about 20 mins), but a little extra for customers further away - see below.

For customers further away, we charge a little extra due to the extra time needed to get to and from you (and the fuel). Usually this is £6 extra per visit, but please [contact us](/contactus/) for a quote.

<h2>Evening and weekend rates</h2>
We try to keep most of our work to Monday to Friday, but of course if a customer is going away, animals still need looking after, so we work weekends and evenings too, but we do charge a little extra. Weekend and evening dog walks are £20 per hour for the first dog (additional dogs from the same household remaining at £10 per dog). Pop-ins are £15 per visit and pet sits are £20 her hour.

<h2>Holidays</h2>
We're trying to adjust our work life balance a little, otherwise we find ourselves working all the time, so we are reserving a weekend off per month from March to October (hoping for nice-ish weather) and a week off during June/July time. Please [contact us](/contactus/) for exact dates.

<h2>Services we are no longer able to offer</h2>
Certain services we are unfortunately no longer able to offer - our schedule is full of dog walking and pop-ins meaning something had to go! Therefore, we are no longer able to offer equestrian related services and overnight pet sits.

If helpful, we have links with other similar businesses in the area, so if we're unable to help, we might be able to point you in the direction of someone who could help, do [get in touch](/contactus/) .
